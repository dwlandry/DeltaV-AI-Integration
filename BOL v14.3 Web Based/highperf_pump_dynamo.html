High Performance Pump dynamo (HP_Pump)

This dynamo can be configured for either a level 1 or level 2 display, or can be customized to include a combination of elements. The table below lists all of the elements that may be present on the HP_Pump dynamo when customizing it, and which elements are available when configuring the dynamo for a level 1 or level 2 display. Click the link to see a description of the element.

Level 1 HP_Pump dynamo Level 2 HP_Pump dynamo
The HP_Pump dynamo has four possible orientations: right, left, up-right, and up-left.
