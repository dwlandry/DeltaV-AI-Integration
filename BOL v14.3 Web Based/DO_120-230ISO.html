DO VAC isolated CHARM specifications

Installation notes

  • When a CHARM Terminal Block is used, the output channel can be used as a conventional isolated output channel.
  • When a CHARM Fused Injected Power Terminal Block is used, the output channel can be configured as a high-side output channel.
  • Although CHARMs can be installed in any location, it is good engineering practice to separate low voltage DC signals from high voltage AC signals. To ensure a safe working environment, it is recommended that you wire AC signals to a different enclosure from DC signals.
  • This CHARM requires snubbers for inductive loads. Refer to the Related information section of this topic for links to additional information.

Specifications

DO VAC isolated CHARM specifications
Item Specification
Channel type AC voltage discrete output: either conventional isolated output or high-side output depending upon the terminal block used.
Configuration options
  • Discrete Output
  • Momentary
  • Continuous pulse output
Output range 20 to 250 VAC
Output rating 0.5 A maximum continuous
On-state output rating 0.5 A continuous (inrush 2.5 A for <100 ms; 10 A for < 20 ms)
Off-state leakage
  • 2 mA maximum at 120 VAC
  • 4 mA maximum at 230 VAC
Channel isolation The output channel is optically isolated at 250 VAC.
Field circuit protection An external fuse at the power source is recommended.
Input power requirements Total power requirement: +24 VDC ±10% at 12 mA maximum :
  • Input power from CIOC: +6.3 VDC ±3% at 32 mA maximum
  • +24 VDC input power: none
CHARM heat dissipation 0.7 W
