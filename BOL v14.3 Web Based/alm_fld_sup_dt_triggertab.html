Trigger Conditions tab (ALM_FLD_SUP_DT)

The following items are contained in the Trigger Conditions tab of the detail display:

  • Required - Displays the process conditions that must be present in order for the module to trip.
  • First out - Indicates the voting condition or conditions that satisfied the required vote count.
  • Votes - Displays the configured voting conditions that must be present in order for the module to trip.
