Locate assigned CHARMs in DeltaV Explorer

The Goto commands provide a fast and easy way to find an assigned CHARM in the controller's hierarchy or a physical CHARM under the I/O Network.

  • To find a CHARM assignment, right-click the configured CHARM under the I/O Network and select Goto CHARM Assignment.

  • To find a configured CHARM, right-click the assigned CHARM and select Goto CHARM.
