Transitions in a PFC

A transition defines when a recipe moves from one step to another and where the recipe stops. The termination transition is the logical end of a PFC. Recipe Studio automatically creates the initial step and initial transition. You must create the termination transition where appropriate. Each transition defines a Boolean condition that must be true before the step that follows it can run or the recipe end.

By default, Recipe Studio initially sets each transition you add to a chart to TRUE. However, frequently you do not want a recipe to move onto the next step until the current step is complete or until a specific condition is met, such as a vessel is charged with all of the necessary ingredients. You can edit the default condition by configuring it for your needs.

A typical condition might be as follows:

'AGITATION/BSTATUS' = 'PHASE_STATE:COMPLETE'

This condition ensures the AGITATE step is complete before executing the next step in the PFC.

You can browse in the Expression Editor dialog to find the correct parameter.
