Inrush (soft start)
  • 12 A peak maximum for 5 ms over the 12 VDC input range (excluding 12 VDC output)
  • 20 A peak maximum for 5 ms over the 24 VDC input range (including 12 VDC outputs)
Input protection Internally fused, non-replaceable fuses
Overvoltage protection Output protected at 110% to 120%
Hold-up time Output remains within 5% of nominal at full load and minimum input voltage for 5 ms (excluding 12 VDC current with 12 VDC input)
Mounting On either slot of the 2-wide power/controller carrier
External connectors
  • Primary power: DC input, 2-wire
  • Alarm contacts: 2-wire normally open relays; relays are closed when 3.3 and 5 VDC outputs are within ±4% of nominal
  • Alarm relay contact rating: 30 VDC at 2.0 A; 250 VAC at 2.0 A
