Commission a CSLS

  1. Right click a CSLS placeholder in the Local Safety Network and select Commission.
  2. On the confirmation dialog that appears, click Yes. The Secure Write confirm dialog appears.
  3. Click Confirm to commission the CSLS. The CSLS icon turns yellow to show that it has been commissioned.
