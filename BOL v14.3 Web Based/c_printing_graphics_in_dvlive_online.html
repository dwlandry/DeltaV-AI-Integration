Printing graphics in DeltaV Live (online)

Both displays and screens can be printed, as they appear, in DeltaV Live online to any printer defined on that workstation or to various types of documents (such as XPS or PDF). When printing displays, you can choose to print the content of a single display frame or open pop-up window. When printing screens, you can choose to print a single screen or all screens in the running layout.

The following options are provided for choosing your print content, scope, and scale of the document:

Print scope

Print Screen x
Select a specific screen to print.
Print All Screens
Print all screens exactly as they appear online.
Print Display x
Select a specific display or contextual display to print.
Pages
Specify the page numbers to print.

Print scale

Scale Down to Fit Paper
Scales the document down to fit the paper with a .2" margin.
Scale to Fit Paper
Scales the document up or down to fit the paper with a .2" margin.
Don't Scale
Prints the document at 100% of its configured size
