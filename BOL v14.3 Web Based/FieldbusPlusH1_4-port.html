Fieldbus H1 4-port Plus card specifications

Installation notes

  • The Simplex H1 4-port Plus Terminal Block is recommended to provide screw terminations for field wiring for the Fieldbus H1 4-port Plus card in simplex mode.
  • The Redundant H1 4-port Plus Terminal Block is recommended to provide screw terminations for field wiring for the Fieldbus H1 4-port card in redundant mode.
  • This card requires an S-series controller.
