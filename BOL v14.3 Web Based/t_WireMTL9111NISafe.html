Connect the MTL NI power supplies to the safe area

Refer to the following image for the location of the safe area connectors on the power supplies.
Connectors on the MTL 9111 and 9112 NI power supplies


  1. Connect the primary power supply positive (+) wire to pin 1.
  2. Connect the primary power negative (-) 24 V return to pin 2.
  3. Connect a secondary power supply positive (optional) (+) wire to pin 3. If using a primary and secondary 24 VDC power supply, be sure to connect the power supply returns together.
  4. Connect the host segment negative (-) to pin 4.
  5. Connect the host segment shield to pin 5.
  6. Connect the host segment positive (+) .
