Dual Universal Carrier specifications

Installation notes

  • The Dual Universal Carrier holds multiple card types and installs on horizontal DIN rails.
  • The redundant Ethernet I/O Card (EIOC) can plug into the slots on the Dual Universal Carrier.
  • The Dual Universal Carrier provides redundant 24 VDC power for the cards. Refer to Powering the Dual Universal Carrier.
  • The Dual Universal Carrier houses a primary and secondary Ethernet Isolation Port (EIP). For the redundant EIOC, each EIP consists of an Area Control Network port for connections to the DeltaV Area Control Network and a Device Network Port for connections to the intelligent device network.

Specifications

Dual Universal Carrier specifications
Item Specification
Capacity One or two redundant cards such as the EIOC
Input power (redundant) +24 VDC ±10% at 1 A maximum
Redundant Ethernet connections (primary and secondary) Copper twisted pair: 10/100BASE-TX with RJ45 connectors; full duplex operation.
