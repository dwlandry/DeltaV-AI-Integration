Connect the CHARMs Smart Logic Solver to the Local Safety Network

It is assumed that the Ethernet cable and switches for the Local Safety Network are installed.

The CSLS connects to the Local Safety Network through the Safety Network ports on the CSLS Carrier.



  1. Connect the primary Safety Network Port on the CSLS Carrier to the primary Local Safety Network switch.
  2. Connect the secondary Safety Network Port on the CSLS Carrier to the secondary Local Safety Network switch.
