DeltaV Zone 1 Intrinsically Safe system hardware

DeltaV Zone 1 Intrinsically Safe system


The DeltaV Zone 1 Intrinsically Safe system consists of the following hardware:

Zone 1 Carrier
Provides connections and hardware for the Zone 1 Power Supply, Zone 1 CPU, and up to four Zone 1 I/O cards. The carrier mounts on a panel in a customer-supplied increased safety enclosure. The enclosure must have the appropriate certifications for the location in which it is installed. The carrier provides the input power screw terminals as well as the field wiring terminals for the I/O cards.
Zone 1 Power Supply
Provides an isolation barrier between incoming power and power supplied to the other units and field circuits in the system. The source voltage is 24 VDC. The power supply, which plugs into the left-most position on the carrier, (labeled PS) is in a flameproof enclosure.
Zone 1 CPU
Performs control, communications, and power conversion for the Intrinsically Safe system. Most of the connections to the CPU are through the carrier. Two fiber-optic Ethernet ports on the CPU provide the communications interface to the DeltaV system. The CPU plugs into the carrier to the right of the power supply in the position labeled CPU. It is in a flameproof enclosure.
Zone 1 Analog I/O card
Provides eight analog input channels and four analog output channels. The analog input channels are Intrinsically Safe 2-wire 4-20 mA with HART. The analog output channels are Intrinsically Safe 4-20 mA with HART.
Zone 1 Discrete I/O card
Provides sixteen discrete input channels and eight discrete output channels. The discrete input channels are Intrinsically Safe dry contact inputs with the first two channels optionally supporting pulse count. The discrete output channels are Intrinsically Safe high side DC outputs.
