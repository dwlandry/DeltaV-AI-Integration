Responding to alarms from the alarm banner display

Alarms are most visible on an alarm banner, which is typically configured to appear at the bottom of the layout in DeltaV Live . An alarm banner is reserved for the highest priority alarms, which are shown in priority order, with the most recent, highest priority alarm on the left.

The importance of an alarm is determined by the following criteria:

  • Unacknowledged alarms are more important than acknowledged alarms.

  • For alarms with equal acknowledgment status, the priority (CRITICAL, WARNING, and ADVISORY) determines the importance. CRITICAL is the most important and ADVISORY is the least important.

  • For alarms with equal acknowledgment status and equal priority status, the controller uses the timestamp to determine the importance. The most recent alarms are the most important.

The horn is not sounded if a low priority alarm is not eligible to be shown on the Alarm Banner. Eligibility is determined by the alarm threshold initialization (gn_ProcessAlarmThreshold) in the UserSettings picture. Refer to The User_Ref and UserSettings Pictures for more information.

Highest priority alarm buttons
The large buttons at the bottom of the Alarm Banner notify you of the highest priority alarms that have been activated. The name of the control module associated with the tripped alarms appears on the button. Click the alarm button to open the associated faceplate and primary control display (if configured) for that alarm. If a primary control display has not been configured for an alarm, a message informs you no primary control display has been configured. The associated buttons on an alarm banner indicate if the faceplate or primary control display has been disabled for the alarm. At least one of these displays must be enabled.
Extended information button
If you click the i button at the right of each alarm, more detailed information about the alarm appears across the bottom of the screen. This information includes the time the alarm occurred, the module description, the alarm parameter, and the alarm priority.
Acknowledge All button
The Acknowledge All button on an alarm banner acknowledges all the alarms in the main window. A faceplate's Acknowledge All button acknowledges all the alarms for that point only.
