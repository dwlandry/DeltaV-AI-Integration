Creating pictures

DeltaV Operate allows developers and process engineers to create dynamic pictures with minimal effort. By providing an open-environment, ActiveX format, DeltaV Operate lets you incorporate internet controls (OCXs), reuse elements from other sources (such as bitmaps), or embed other OLE automation applications into your pictures.

While creating pictures in DeltaV Operate, you can utilize features that give you the control you need to customize your drawings. You can view your pictures by merely switching to the run-time environment with the click of a mouse.
