DeltaV H1 cards

The DeltaV system supports simplex and redundant H1 cards and simplex and redundant H1 cards with Integrated Power.

In this document, H1 card refers to the following products:
  • Simplex and redundant S-series H1 card
  • Simplex and redundant Series 2 H1 card
  • Simplex and redundant Series 2 H1 4-port Plus card
  • Simplex and redundant S-series H1 4-port Plus card
In this document, H1 card with Integrated Power refers to the following products:
  • Simplex and redundant S-series H1 card with Integrated Power

The H1 card requires external fieldbus power supplies to power the fieldbus devices. The H1 with Integrated Power card provides the fieldbus power required for most applications. Emerson recommends the use of the H1 with Integrated Power card for most fieldbus applications because it requires less cabinet space and wiring, resulting in lower installation costs. In addition, the H1 with Integrated Power card provides individual integrated segment power diagnostics to complement existing hardware alerts and segment communication statistics without additional components or wiring.
