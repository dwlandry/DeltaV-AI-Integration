Installing a Redundant Terminal Block

Read the information in “DeltaV Series 2 I/O” before installing a redundant terminal block.

  1. Check the key settings on the corresponding Series 2 cards and set the keys on the terminal block to match. Refer to “I/O Interface Keying” for information on key settings.
  2. Locate the assigned slot location on the I/O interface carrier. Remember that the lower slot number must be odd and the upper slot number must be the next higher even number. Place the tabs on the back of the I/O terminal block through the slots on the carrier and push the I/O terminal block up to lock it into place.
  3. Connect the field wiring for the redundant I/O terminal blocks as shown in the Series 2 card wiring diagrams and redundant terminal block figures in the "Interface Specifications" section.
