CHARM I/O Carrier specifications

Installation notes

  • The CHARM I/O Carrier installs on vertical T-type DIN rails.
  • Simplex or redundant CHARM I/O Cards (CIOCs) plug into the slots on the CHARM I/O Carrier.
  • The CHARM I/O Carrier provides redundant 24 VDC power for the CIOC and bussed power for the field circuits. Refer to the image Powering the CHARM I/O Carrier for information on powering the CHARM I/O Carrier.
  • The CHARM I/O Carrier houses a primary and secondary I/O Port (IOP). There are two types of IOPs: a copper IOP and a fiber-optic IOP. The copper IOP consists of a control network port and a cascade port. The fiber-optic IOP consists of a control network port only. The I/O ports are removeable. To remove an I/O port, depress the button next to the I/O port and pull the I/O port out of the carrier. Refer to the image Connectors on the CHARM I/O Carrier for the location of the IOPs on the carrier. The image shows a carrier with copper IOPs.
  • Copper Ethernet cascade ports (primary and secondary) on the CIOC I/O Carrier allow up to 16 CIOCs to be connected together (daisy-chained) without wiring back to a central switch. Fiber-optic I/O ports can be used for remotely located CIOCS. CIOCs with a fiber-optic port cannot be daisy-chained and are used in star-connected networks.
  • Redundant CIOCs connect to the control network through primary and secondary ethernet connections on the CIOC I/O Carrier.

Specifications

CHARM I/O Carrier specifications
Item Specification
Capacity One or two redundant CHARM I/O cards
Input power (redundant) +24 VDC ±10% at 12 A maximum
Output power to CHARMs +6.3 VDC at 3.25 A
Redundant Ethernet connections (primary and secondary)
  • Fiber-optic: 100BASE-FX with MTRJ connectors; full duplex; multimode; 1300 nm; 2 km nominal distance.
  • Cat. 5(e) screened, twisted pair cable: 10/100BASE-TX with RJ45 connectors; half or full duplex operation
Fiber-optic link budget

62.5/125 um:

  • Transmit power minimum: 20 dBm
  • Receive sensitivity maximum: -31 dBm
  • Attenuation maximum: 11 dB
  • Input power maximum: -14 dBm

50/125 um:

  • Transmit power minimum: -23 dBm
  • Receive sensitivity maximum: -31 dBm
  • Attenuation maximum: 8 dB
  • Input power maximum: -14 dBm
