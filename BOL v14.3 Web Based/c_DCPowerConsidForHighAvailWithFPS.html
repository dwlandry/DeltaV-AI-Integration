DC power considerations for high availability applications that use the H1 with Integrated Power card

The available power to a field device depends on the length and resistance characteristics of the fieldbus cable. The table in the topic Maximum distances per load on the H1 with Integrated Power card shows the allowable distances per load with Megablocks. The formula for the calculations in the table is based on the following assumptions:

  • Power supply voltage = 28.0 VDC @ 400 mA
  • Minimum device voltage = 9 VDC (calculations use 11 VDC at input to Megablock)
  • Maximum voltage drop from cable = 17 VDC
  • Each device has an average load of 20 mA (includes the coupler)
  • Fieldbus Type A 18 AWG cable @ 22 Ω/km (44 Ω/km loop resistance) at 22°C
  • Devices are connected on the opposite end of the cable from the power supply
  • Maximum distance (km) = (allowed loop V drop / loop current) / loop resistance per km

There will be different restrictions and limitations on your segment if these assumptions do not hold for your segment layout. If your devices average more than 20 mA per device, reduce the maximum cable length indicated in the table for that number of devices or reduce the number of devices on the segment. Refer to the device documentation for information on current requirements for the device.
