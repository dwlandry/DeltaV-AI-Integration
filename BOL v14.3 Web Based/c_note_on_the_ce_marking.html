Note on the CE marking

The devices comply with the regulations contained in the following European directives:

2004/108/EMC Directive, 2011/65/EU (ROHS Directive): FP20/FP20-ES switches and MD20/30 and MD20/30-ES switches

2004/108/EMC Directive, 2006/95/EC, 2011/65/EU (ROHS Directive): RM100/RM104 switches

Directive of the European Parliament and the Council for standardizing the regulations of member states on electromagnetic compatibility. In accordance with the above-named EU directives, the EU conformity declaration will be at the disposal of the relevant authorities at the following address:

Emerson, Meridian East, Meridian East Business Park, Leicester, LE19 1X, UK

  • Interference immunity: EN 61000-6-2:2005
  • Emitted interference: EN 55022:2010 Class A (MD20/MD30, MD20-ES, MD30-ES and RM100/RM104 switches)

This is a class A device. This device can cause interference in living areas, and in this case the operator may be required to take appropriate measures. The assembly guidelines must be strictly adhered to in order to observe the EMC value limits.
