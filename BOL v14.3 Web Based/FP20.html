DeltaV FP20 Smart Switches

Installation notes

  • DeltaV FP20 Smart Switches have eight ports.
  • Two ports on each switch are fiber uplink, twisted pair uplink, or a combination of fiber and twisted pair uplink ports. The remaining 6 ports are 10/100mb/s twisted pair.
