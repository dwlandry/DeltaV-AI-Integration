8-Wide Horizontal I/O Interface Carrier

Item Specification
Capacity Eight I/O cards with terminal blocks.
Maximum current

LocalBus (powers I/O cards): 8 A. For large systems use the LocalBus extenders to add more power.

Bussed field power bus (shared by multiple I/O cards): 6.5 A @ 30 VDC or 250 VAC for each connection

LocalBus cable lengths
  • .87 m (2.8 ft)
  • 1.2 m (3.9 ft) (standard)
  • 1.53 m (5.0 ft)
