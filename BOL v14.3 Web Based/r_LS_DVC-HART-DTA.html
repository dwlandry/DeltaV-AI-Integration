LS DVC HART DTA CHARMs specifications

Installation notes

The DeltaV system supports the LS DVC HART DTA CHARM, and the LS DVC HART (Redundant DTA) CHARM. Redundancy allows for higher safety coverage to shutdown the process.

  • The LS DVC HART DTA CHARM requires the LS DVC Terminal Block. This CHARM is suitable for use in SIL 3 de-energize to trip (DTA) applications.
  • The LS DVC HART (Redundant DTA) CHARM requires the LS Redundant DVC Terminal Block. This CHARM is suitable for use in SIL 3 de-energize to trip (DTA) applications.

Specifications

LS DVC HART DTA CHARMs specifications
Item Specification
Channel type Analog output: 4-20 mA with HART
Nominal signal range
  • 4-20 mA
  • 0-20 mA optional
Full signal range 0-24 mA
Safety/diagnostic accuracy 5% of span
Resolution 12 bits
Calibration None required
Compliance voltage
  • Voltage to load: 15 V minimum at 20 mA and 24 VDC
  • Load resistance: 250 Ω minimum to 600 Ω maximum
Input power requirements

Total power requirement (simplex): +24 VDC ±10% at 56 mA maximum:

  • Input power from CSLS Power Modules:+6.3 VDC ± 3% at 60 mA maximum
  • 24 VDC input power: +24 VDC ±10% at 36 mA maximum

Total power requirement (redundant pair): +24 VDC ±10% at 86 mA maximum:

  • Input power from CSLS Power Modules:+6.3 VDC ± 3% at 120 mA maximum
  • 24 VDC input power: +24 VDC ±10% at 46 mA maximum
CHARM heat dissipation 1.03 W
HART scan time

Typically less than one second but is dependent on HART communication loading and the capability of the HART device.

HART communications
  • HART pass-through request/response
  • HART variable report
  • Field device status report
