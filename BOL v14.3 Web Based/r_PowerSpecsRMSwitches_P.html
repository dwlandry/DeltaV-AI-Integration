Power Specifications for the DeltaV RM100 Smart Switches

Specifications

The following table provides power and other specifications for the DeltaV RM100 Smart Switches.

Specifications for DeltaV RM100 Smart Switches
Item Specification
Input voltage 100 - 240 VAC
Input current 0.4 - 0.2 A
Mounting Horizontally on a 19 inch rack or vertically on the wall

Rear View

The RM100 Base Smart Switch-RP supports redundant input power. The power supply inputs are uncoupled. The following figure shows the redundant power connections on the rear of the RM100 Base Smart Switch-RP.

Redundant Power on the RM100 Base Smart Switch-RP


The standard power supply alone supplies power to the device. The redundant power supply automatically becomes active if the standard supply fails.
