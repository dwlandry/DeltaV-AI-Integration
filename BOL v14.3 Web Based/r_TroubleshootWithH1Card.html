Using the LEDs to troubleshoot

The LEDs on the H1 and H1 with Integrated Power cards provide a great deal of information for troubleshooting fieldbus segments. Once communication has been established, use the LEDs to monitor communication between the card and fieldbus devices. The bottom two LEDs on the cards reflect communication between the port and fieldbus devices on that port.
