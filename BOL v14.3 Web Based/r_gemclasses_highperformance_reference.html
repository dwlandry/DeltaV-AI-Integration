High Performance GEM classes reference

To support ISA's display optimization recommendations, the high performance GEM classes (High_Performance_Gems) are designed to improve operators' situational awareness on level 1 and 2 displays. These GEMs present information through consistent, repeated patterns so that operators can more easily identify deviations from the target. They are also designed to support emergent patterns by grouping or stacking several GEMs together.
