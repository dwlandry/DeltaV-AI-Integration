CHARM Address Terminal specifications

Installation notes

  • The CHARM Address Terminal is preinstalled in the dedicated slot on the CHARM Baseplate and holds one Address Plug (1-8).
  • The CHARM Address Terminal's keying mechanism sets itself to match the inserted Address Plug (1-8).
  • To reset the keying mechanism, remove the Address Plug (1-8) from the CHARM Baseplate, remove the Address Terminal, and reset the keying mechanism.
  • Four screw terminals on the CHARM Address Terminal provide field power to isolated LS CHARMs installed in CHARM Fused Injected Power Terminal Blocks.
