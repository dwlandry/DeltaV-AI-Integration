LS AI 4-20 mA HART (Intrinsically Safe) CHARM specifications

Installation notes

  • The LS AI 4-20 mA HART (I.S.) CHARM requires the I.S. CHARM Terminal Block.
  • The LS AI 4-20 mA HART (I.S.) CHARM supports 2 and 4-wire devices.

Specifications

LS AI 4-20 mA HART (I.S.) CHARM specifications
Item Specification
Channel type and functionality Intrinsically safe analog input:
  • 4-20 mA with HART
  • 0-20 mA
Nominal signal range (span)
  • 4-20 mA
  • 0-20 mA (optional)
Full signal range 0-22 mA
Input impedance 360 Ω
Input offset voltage 2.0 VDC (maximum)
2-wire transmitter power
  • 16.0 VDC minimum at 20 mA
  • Current limit: 25 mA (typical)
Accuracy over temperature range
  • 0.1% of span (0 to 60° C)
  • .25% of span (-40°C to 70°C)
Repeatability 0.05% of span
Resolution 16-bit
Calibration None required
Channel isolation Galvanically isolated according to EN 60079-11, voltage peak value 375 V.
Input power requirements

+24 VDC input power: +24 VDC ±10% at 60 mA maximum

CHARM heat dissipation (includes terminal block) 0.86 W
HART scan time Typically less than one second but is dependent on HART communication loading and the capability of the HART device.
HART Communications
  • HART pass-through request/response
  • HART variable report
  • Field device status report
