Responding to alarms from the Alarm Mosaic application

Alarm Mosaic is a real-time alarm-monitoring application that provides operators with a way to identify problems in the running process control system, giving them time to take corrective actions. The alarms displayed in the application are the same as the alarms displayed in the DeltaV Operate or DeltaV Live Alarm Banner and are represented as icons. A maximum of 500 alarms can be displayed on the timeline at one time. The alarms are presented both graphically and in list form.

Many of the alarm actions that can be done in the DeltaV Live Alarm List display are also supported in Alarm Mosaic, such as:

  • Acknowledging alarms

  • Opening call-ups, detail displays, and faceplates

  • Opening Alarm Help
