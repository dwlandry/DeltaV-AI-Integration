WirelessHART I/O Card specifications

Installation notes

  • The WirelessHART I/O Card (WIOC) installs on the WirelessHART I/O Carrier. The WIOC supports redundancy.
  • WIOCs require the redundant WirelessHART I/O Energy Limited Terminal Block or the WirelessHART I/O Terminal Block for terminations for wiring to simplex or redundant Smart Wireless Field Links.
  • WIOCs receive redundant 24 VDC power and condition it to power the Smart Wireless Field Link through the terminal block on the Wireless I/O Carrier.
  • Simplex or redundant WIOCs connect to the control network through primary and secondary ethernet connections on the WirelessHART I/O Carrier.
  • Ethernet cascade ports on the WIOC Carrier allow up to 16 WIOCs to be connected together without wiring back to a central switch.
  • The WIOC communicates with the DeltaV controller through the Wireless I/O Carrier.

Specifications

WirelessHART I/O Card specifications
Item Specification
Input power per WirelessHART I/O card +24 VDC ± 10% at 400 mA maximum
Output power to the Smart Wireless Field Link through the Wireless I/O carrier +24 VDC at 100 mA maximum
Ethernet connection through the Wireless I/O carrier Redundant (primary and secondary) 10/100BASE-TX and 100BASE-FX ports capable of full-duplex operation.
WIOC heat dissipation 11 W maximum per redundant WIOC node:
  • 3.5 W for each WIOC
  • 2 W for each fiber-optic I/O port (IOP); 1.34 W for each copper I/O port
HART scan time 8 seconds for 100 devices
Isolation None. The WIOC is referenced to the incoming 24 VDC return.
Mounting One or two slots on the Wireless I/O Carrier.
