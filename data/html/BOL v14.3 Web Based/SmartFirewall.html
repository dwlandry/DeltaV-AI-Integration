Emerson Smart Firewall specifications

The Emerson Smart Firewall controls traffic going into the DeltaV network; in particular HTTP, OPC, and remote desktop traffic. It also controls traffic coming out of the DeltaV network such as HTTP.

The Safety instructions for the Emerson Smart Firewall can be found in DeltaV Books Online.

Installation notes

  • The firewall can be installed between the DeltaV network and a plant LAN and between the DeltaV network, a demilitarized zone (DMZ), and the plant LAN.
  • Read the safety instructions for the Emerson Smart Firewall before connecting or using the firewall. Refer to Books online for the Emerson Smart Firewall Safety Instructions.
