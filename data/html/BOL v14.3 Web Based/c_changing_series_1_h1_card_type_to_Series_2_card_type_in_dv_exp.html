Changing Series 1 H1 card type to Series 2 card type in DeltaV Explorer

When upgrading from Series 1 H1 cards to Series 2 H1 cards and changing the card type in DeltaV Explorer, it is necessary to perform a partial download of the controller for the changes to take affect. A partial download is not required when swapping a Series 1 card with a Series 2 card for repair/replacement purposes. The download is required only when the database is changed. Follow these steps to upgrade the card:

  1. Replace the card.

  2. Open DeltaV Explorer, select the H1 card, and then select Properties from the context menu.

  3. Change the Card Series from Series 1 to Series 2.

  4. Download any control modules that are flagged with the blue triangle icon .

  5. Download the Series 2 card.

    Tip:

    If multiple modules are flagged with the blue triangle icon, you can save time during the download by selecting the Uncheck All button on the Select Additional Objects to Download dialog.

  6. Download any fieldbus devices flagged with the blue triangle. Blue triangles should not appear on the port or on any control modules.
