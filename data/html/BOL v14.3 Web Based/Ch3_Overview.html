Checking Out and Troubleshooting Your DeltaV System

This chapter provides guidelines to help ensure that your system is installed properly and to help you troubleshoot hardware problems. The System Administration and Maintenance manual in Books Online and the Release Notes KBA provide updated maintenance and troubleshooting information.

After installing, it is recommended that you check out the hardware to ensure a smooth startup.

After checkout, start up the DeltaV software following the instructions in the manual Getting Started with Your DeltaV Software. You can then verify and troubleshoot your hardware installation by using:

  • DeltaV Explorer to view the overall structure and layout of your system
  • Diagnostics utilities to check workstation hardware
  • DeltaV Diagnostics to view diagnostics data for DeltaV hardware
