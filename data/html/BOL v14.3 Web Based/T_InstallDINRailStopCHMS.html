Install a DIN Rail Stop

Vibration may cause baseplates to slip down on the DIN rail. Install a DIN Rail Stop after the Column Terminator to prevent slippage.

DIN Rail Stop


  1. Be sure the arrow on the top of the DIN Rail Stop is pointing upwards. This prevents potential damage from the screws on the stop.
  2. Hook the end with the deeper notch over the DIN rail and then hook the end with the more shallow notch over the other side of the DIN rail.
  3. Screw the stop onto the DIN rail being careful not to overtighten the screws. Be sure that both sides of the DIN rail stop are properly secured. The torque limits are specified on the DIN rail stop.
