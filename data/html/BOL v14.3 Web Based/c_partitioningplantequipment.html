Partitioning plant equipment

In addition to identifying the process flow, you also need to partition your plant equipment. This involves an equipment hierarchy composed of process cells, equipment trains, units, and low level control modules. Partitioning your equipment for maximum efficiency is key to a successful system.

Issues to consider include:

  • Batch paths

  • Shared equipment

  • Common resources

  • Operator control

  • DeltaV process control

Typically, a process and instrumentation diagram (P&ID) is used to identify and partition the equipment used in a process.

Prior to configuring equipment in DeltaV Explorer, partition your equipment for maximum productivity. This includes identifying:

  • The equipment that makes up an equipment train.

  • The equipment and control modules that make up a unit

  • Common resources

To achieve the most efficient and easily maintained equipment, use the following guidelines:

  • Define equipment without a particular product in mind. This way, when you make a change in the product or process, it is less likely you will need to change the equipment definitions.

  • Define each piece of equipment to work independently.
