I.S. CHARM Baseplate specifications

Installation notes

  • The I.S. CHARM Baseplates hold LS I.S. CHARMs and install directly onto a vertical T-type DIN rail. I.S. CHARM Baseplates connect to the CSLS carrier and to other I.S. CHARM Baseplates through connectors on the baseplates.
  • A Separation Wall must be installed on every I.S. CHARM Baseplate.
  • Each I.S. CHARM Baseplate holds one Address plug and up to 12 I.S. CHARM terminal blocks holding LS I.S. CHARMs.
  • Connect the I.S. CHARM Baseplate with Address Plug 1 to the CSLS carrier as shown in Connected I.S. CHARM Baseplates.
  • Install I.S. CHARM Baseplates in sequential order under the CSLS carrier based on the Address Plug installed on the baseplate.
  • Up to eight I.S. CHARM Baseplates can be installed under one CSLS carrier.

I.S. CHARM Baseplate specifications
Item Specification
Total +24 VDC power
  • +24 VDC ± 10% at 7.6 A maximum

  • One address plug (1-8) installed in an I.S. CHARM address terminal.
