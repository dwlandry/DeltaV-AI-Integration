DeltaV MD20 and MD30 Base Smart Switches

Installation notes

  • The MD20 and MD30 Base Smart Switches consist of a base switch and backplane. The base switch functions as the power supply and the backplane houses the switching circuits.
  • The MD media modules plug into the slots on the backplane and provide the interfaces to the DeltaV Control Network in a variety of media types.
  • All media modules are hot pluggable.
