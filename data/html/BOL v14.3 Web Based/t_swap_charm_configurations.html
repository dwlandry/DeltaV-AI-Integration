Swap CHARM configurations

Suppose that your design changed, and as a result either the wiring to a CHARM or a CHARM's configuration must change. The Swap CHARMs Configuration command provides a fast and easy way to respond to late design changes or mistakes in configuration or installation by swapping configuration between two CHARMs.

  1. In the DeltaV Explorer, select the CHARMs container under the CIOC. The CIOC's CHARMs are listed in the right pane.
  2. Select the two CHARMs to swap and click Swap CHARMs Configuration from the context menu.
