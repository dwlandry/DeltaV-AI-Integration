Redundant AI 8-Channel Terminal Block (2 or 4-wire) specifications

Installation notes

  • This terminal block is used to terminate wiring for the S-series Redundant AI 8-channel 4-20 mA HART card. Set the terminal block key to A1 before installing the card.
  • This terminal block can be used with 2 or 4-wire transmitters or with a combination of 2 and 4-wire transmitters.

Specifications

Redundant AI 8-Channel Terminal Block (2 or 4-wire) specifications
Item Specification
Voltage rating 30 VDC
Maximum current 200 mA
Operating temperature -40°C to 70°C (-40°F to 158°F)
Mounting Assigned slots of I/O carrier. The lower slot number must be odd and the upper slot number must be the next higher even number. For example, slots 1 and 2, slots 3 and 4, and slots 5 and 6 are valid pairs. Slots 2 and 3 are not a valid pair.
