Connecting Power to the System

A bulk power supply converts AC or DC power to the power required for the system power supply and, optionally, for field devices. The method of connecting power to the DeltaV system depends on your existing power supply, power distribution scheme, and grounding techniques. Refer to the DeltaV Power and Grounding manual for recommended power and grounding techniques for a DeltaV system.
