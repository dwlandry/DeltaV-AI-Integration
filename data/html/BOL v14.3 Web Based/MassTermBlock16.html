16-Pin Mass Terminal Block specifications

Installation notes

The 16-Pin Mass Terminal block provides 1:1 signal transmission of eight signals to the external passive input/output modules. The two-row header accepts a 16-pin 1-for-1 passthrough, 0.093 mm 2- ribbon cable (28 AWG) with 2x8 header connectors (AMP part #1658622-3, Strain Relief #499252-8). The assembly interfaces to an IDC-to-discrete-wire module, such as the Phoenix Contact 2304432 FLKM16 VARIOFACE ribbon connector module, or other similar modules. It can also interface to the HiD Series Boards from Pepperl+Fuchs if IS Barriers are required. The Phoenix Contact 2304432 FLKM16 VARIOFACE ribbon connector module and the Pepperl+Fuchs HiD Series Boards are Emerson Alliance Program products.
