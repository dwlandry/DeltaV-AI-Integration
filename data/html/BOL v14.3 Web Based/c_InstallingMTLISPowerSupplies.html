Installing the MTL Intrinsically Safe power supplies

The MTL9121 and 9122 Intrinsically Safe power supplies install on a DIN Rail and are powered by a typical bulk 24 VDC power supply. The IS power supplies connect to the segment wiring to power the fieldbus devices that are not self-powered. On the top of each supply is a 3 pin connector for connection to the safe area host fieldbus and a 3 pin connector for connection to the safe area 24 V power. On the bottom of each supply is a 3 pin connector for connection to the IS area. The following figure shows the connectors on the power supplies.
