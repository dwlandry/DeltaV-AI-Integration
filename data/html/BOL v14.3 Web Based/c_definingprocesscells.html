Defining process cells

A process cell is a logical grouping of equipment that includes the equipment required for production of one or more batches. Depending on how your equipment is partitioned, a process cell can include one or more production lines, which are used to produce more than one product.

Recipes are built and executed based on the equipment defined in a process cell.  Process cells are associated with particular areas. Similarly, areas are associated with a specific Batch Executive through an assignment to a Batch Executive. A plant area can be assigned to only one Batch Executive at a time. There can be up to four enabled Batch Executives in a DeltaV system.

Process cells exist under System Configuration/Control Strategies/Areas in the DeltaV Explorer hierarchy.
