DeltaV FP20 Smart Switches

Specifications

The DeltaV FP20 Smart Switches are all 8-port switches. Each of the FP20 Smart Switches has 6, 10/100Mbit twisted pair ports, two of the eight ports on each switch are fiber uplink ports, twisted pair uplink ports, or a combination of fiber and twisted pair uplink ports.
