Manually decommission and unassign a CHARMs Smart Logic Solver

Performing these manual steps resets the CSLS and the non-volatile memory within the Safety Network Ports to the default unassigned state.

  1. Unplug the primary and secondary Safety Network Ports from the carrier.
  2. Remove both CSLSs from the carrier and install them in the opposite slots: move the CSLS in the right slot to the left slot and the CSLS in the left slot to the right slot.
  3. Wait one to two minutes for the CSLS to boot up.
  4. Plug the primary and secondary Safety Network Ports into their original locations.
    Note: This procedure can be useful in the rare case that a new database has been brought online or the CSLS has been removed from one Local Safety Network and placed on another Local Safety Network before it was unassigned.
