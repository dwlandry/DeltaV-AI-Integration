Dual Universal Safety Carrier specifications

Installation notes

  • The Dual Universal Safety Carrier installs on horizontal DIN rails.
  • Redundant Local Safety Network Bridges or SZ Controllers plug into the slots on the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • The Dual Universal Safety Carrier provides redundant 24 VDC power for Local Safety Network Bridges or SZ Controllers. Refer to Powering the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • The Dual Universal Safety Carrier houses a primary and secondary Ethernet Isolation Port (EIP). When a Local Safety Network Bridge is plugged into the carrier, the EIP consists of a Global Safety Network Port for connections to the Global Safety Network and a Local Safety Network port for connections to the Local Safety Network. When an SZ Controller is plugged into the carrier, the EIP consists of an Area Control Network port for connections to the DeltaV Area Control Network and a Local Safety Network Port for connections to the Local Safety Network. When an SZ Controller is used in a standalone safety system, the Area Control Network ports function as redundant Modbus TCP ports. Each EIP has a port activity LED. The EIPs are removable. To remove an EIP, depress the button next to the EIP and push it out of the carrier. Refer to the images following the table.
  • Screw terminal connections on the carrier allow an external power source to be connected to backup the real-time-clock on the SZ Controllers for power outages greater than 100 hours. If these connectors are used, the power source must meet the requirements in the following table.
