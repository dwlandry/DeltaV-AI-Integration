Multifunction

The DeltaV system supports the Multifunction card and the Series 2 Multifunction card in Simplex mode.

The Multifunction card uses the 32-channel terminal block to provide terminations for wiring. The following table lists the cable pin out connections for the Multifunction card.

The Multifunction I/O Card discrete input channel has a switching hysteresis of 80 mV. Noise signals above this amplitude are detected by the input channel.
