Uninstall the DeltaV Excel Add-in

The DeltaV software and the DeltaV Excel Add-in versions must match. Therefore, you must uninstall the DeltaV Excel Add-in whenever you uninstall or upgrade DeltaV. You can then reinstall the DeltaV Excel Add-in after you reinstall or upgrade DeltaV.

Tip: Uninstall the DeltaV Excel Add-in before uninstalling or upgrading DeltaV and deleting DeltaV folders.
  1. Open Control Panel, and then select Programs and Features.
  2. From the list of installed programs, select ExcelAddInRuntime.
  3. Click Uninstall.
