Power LED (red)1

Slow flashing for 5 seconds

The physical key switch is in the unlock position for more than 5 seconds but less than 10 seconds. The controller is in the Controller Unlock Allowed state.

Steady (stops flashing) and then Off

The physical key switch is in the unlock position for more than 10 seconds. The controller is in the Controller Unlock Allowed state.

Fast flashing for 5 seconds

The physical key switch was returned to the locked position within the 5 seconds of slow flashing. The controller is in the Single Unlock Allowed state.

1 You can also enter these states by removing the left power module and replacing it when the LED is slowly flashing or stopped flashing.
