DeltaV Fiber Switches LEDs

The following tables describe the LEDs on the Fiber Switches.

Fiber Switches' Power LEDs
LED Correct Operating Condition Fault Indication Possible Cause Corrective Action
Green – Power On Off 1. System power is not supplied to unit. 1. Check supply power and connections.
2. Internal fault. 2. Contact technical support.
Red – Error Off On (continuous) Internal fault. Contact technical support.
