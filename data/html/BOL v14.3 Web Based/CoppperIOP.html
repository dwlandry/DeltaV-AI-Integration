Copper I/O Port specifications

Installation notes

  • The CHARM I/O Carrier and the WirelessHART I/O Carrier can house either a copper or fiber-optic I/O port (IOP). The copper IOP consists of a primary and secondary control network port and a primary and secondary cascade port. The fiber-optic IOP consists of a control network port only.
  • The cascade ports on the copper IOP allow up to 16 WirelessHART I/O Carriers or CHARM I/O Carriers to be connected together (daisy-chained) without wiring back to a central switch. The control network ports on the copper IOP connect to the DeltaV Control Network .
  • The IOPs are removeable. To remove an IOP, depress the button next to the IOP and pull it out of the carrier.
