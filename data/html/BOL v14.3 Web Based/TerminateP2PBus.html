Terminate the local peer bus

The local peer bus is terminated by 120 ohm BNC terminators on the right one-wide carrier connected to the last 8-wide carrier.

Important: Be sure to use BNC terminators numbered KJ4010X1-BN1.
  1. Install a one-wide carrier onto the right side of the last carrier.
  2. Place a 120 ohms BNC terminator onto each BNC connector on the carrier and push and turn to lock the terminator into place. Terminate both connectors.
