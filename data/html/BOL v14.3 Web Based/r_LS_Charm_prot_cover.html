LS CHARM Protection Cover

Installation notes

  • The LS CHARM Protection Cover installs in any LS CHARM Terminal Block and is used to protect an unused LS CHARM Terminal Block from damage. The LS CHARM Protection Cover does not affect the key setting on the LS CHARM Terminal Block.
  • To install the LS CHARM Protection Cover, open the latch on the LS CHARM Terminal Block, insert the CHARM Protection Cover, and push the cover until the latch closes.
