Connect a remote peer ring with multimode fiber

Connectors on the SISNet Repeater (Bottom View)


  1. Place one end of the fiber-optic cable connector on the input terminal (RxD) and push and turn the cable connector into place.
  2. Place the other end of the fiber-optic cable on the output terminal (TxD) of the successor SISNet Repeater and push and turn the cable connector into place.
  3. Connect straight-through or crossover cable (100 meter maximum) to the SISNet Bridge Port.
    Note: To prevent ground loops, build this cable assembly with a shielded, metal-enclosed RJ45 connector on one end and an isolated, plastic-enclosed RJ45 connector on the other end. The metal connector end of the cable assembly must be connected to an Ethernet switch and the plastic end must be connected to the SISNet Bridge Port.
  4. Connect together all SISNet Repeaters in a counter-rotation topology.
