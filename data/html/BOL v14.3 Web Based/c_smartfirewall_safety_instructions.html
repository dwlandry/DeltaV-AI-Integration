Safety instructions for the Emerson Smart Firewall

Product safety

The Emerson Smart Firewall complies with product safety requirements for the United States, Canada, the European Union and Russia.

General safety instructions

The Emerson Smart Firewall is electrically operated. Connect only to AC power supplied according to appropriate national requirements.

This product is intended for installation and operation only by personnel who have received appropriate training for its use.

Use only undamaged parts.

Use the Emerson Smart Firewall only in the manner indicated. To ensure safety, pay attention to all warnings and information relating to safety.

Only qualified service personnel should install or service the Emerson Smart Firewall. All power cords must be disconnected before opening the chassis or servicing the unit.

This product contains Light Emitting Diode (LED) or Laser components that comply with the IEC 60825-1 (2001) requirements: Class1 Laser Product. Light Emitting Diode - Class 1 LED Product.

National and international safety regulations

Ensure the electrical installation meets all local or nationally applicable safety regulations.

Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) guidelines

The Emerson Smart Firewall contains components that are electrostatically sensitive. Inappropriate handling can destroy these components, cause latent, intermittent failures, or reduce the product lifecycle.

Adhere to the following protection measures for ESD sensitive assemblies before servicing or touching any components inside the Emerson Smart Firewall:

  • Use properly sealed, conductive bags to transport ESD sensitive components.
  • Use an appropriate method of ESD protection (such as an ESD wristband connected to ground, ESD straps or ESD footwear on an ESD floor) to create electrical equipotential bonding between yourself and your working environment.

Once you are properly protected from ESD hazards, you can begin to service ESD sensitive components.

CE marking

The Emerson Smart Firewall complies with all applicable directives and regulations required for CE marking and import into the European Union, including the following:

European (EU) directives:

  • 2004/108/EC - Electro-Magnetic Compatibility Directive
  • 2006/95/EC - Low Voltage Directive
  • 2011/65/EU - RoHS recast Directive
  • REACH Regulation

In accordance with the preceding EU directives, an EU Declaration of Conformity has been issued and is available for the Emerson Smart Firewall.

The Emerson Smart Firewall complies with the following European standards:

  • Safety: EN60950-1:2006 +A1: 2010 + A11:2009 + A12:2011
  • Immunity: EN 55024:2010
  • Emissions: EN55022:2010

Electro-Magnetic Compatibility

This is a Class A device. This device can be used in living areas, places of business, and in industrial areas. This device can cause interference in living areas, and in this case the operator may be required to take appropriate measures. The assembly guidelines must be strictly adhered to in order to observe the EMC value limits.

The Emerson Smart Firewall complies with EMC requirements for Australia and New Zealand necessary for C-Tick marking.

FCC note

The Emerson Smart Firewall has been appropriately tested to ensure compliance with Part 15 of the FCC Rules. Operation is subject to the following two conditions: (1) this device may not cause harmful interference, and (2) this device must accept any interference received, including interference that may cause undesired operation.

Marine Certification

The Emerson Smart Firewall complies with the requirements of DNV 2.4 - Marine Certification for shipboard installations.

GOST-R certification

The Emerson Smart Firewall is in compliance with Russian quality standards (GOST-R) that are intended to exclude potentially hazardous or unsafe products from entering Russia's commercial space.

Recycling note

Properly dispose of the Emerson Smart Firewall as electronic waste in accordance with the current disposal regulations of your country, state, or county.
