Legacy Vertical Carriers

DeltaV Legacy Vertical carriers mount on standard 35 mm. (1.38 in.) T- or G-type DIN rails. for more information on vertical DIN rails.

There are 4 types of Legacy Vertical carriers for mounting vertically in a cabinet:

  • Two types of 4-wide power/controller carriers
  • Two types of 8-wide I/O interface carriers

There are two separate cable lengths for connecting the 8-wide Legacy Vertical I/O interface carriers:

  • 1 meter bottom cable extender
  • 2 meter top cable extender

If you are installing Legacy Vertical carriers in high vibration areas, it is highly recommended that you install a DIN rail stop to prevent the carriers from slipping down on the DIN rails.
