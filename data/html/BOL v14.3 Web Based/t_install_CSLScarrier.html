Install a CHARMs Smart Logic Solver Carrier on the DIN rail

The CHARMs Smart Logic Solver Carrier holds redundant CHARMs Smart Logic Solvers (CSLS) and redundant Power Modules. The CSLS and Power Modules receive 24 VDC input power through the carrier. The Power Modules generate 6.3 VDC power for the LS CHARMs. The carrier houses primary and secondary ethernet ports for connections to the Local Safety Network.

No tools are required to install the CSLS Carrier on the DIN rail. A latch on the back of the carrier snaps it into place on the DIN rail. Connectors on the bottom of the carrier connect with connectors on the first CHARM Baseplate.

CSLS Carrier


Align the carrier against a T-type DIN rail and push it to snap it into place.
