CSLS Global Safety Networks

In CSLS safety installations consisting of more than one local safety network (LSN) it may be necessary for a CSLS to access information on another LSN. The global safety network (GSN) provides this capability. Local safety network bridges (LSNBs) are the hardware items that are connected to create the GSN.

The following table lists terms and acronyms used to describe CSLS global safety network components.

Terms and acronyms
Term Meaning

LSNB

Local Safety Network Bridge

The LSNB is on the LSN under the SIS Controller. It collects user-configured secure parameters from the CSLSs on the LSN it is connected to and publishes them on the GSN. The LSNB also receives data from other LSNBs on the GSN. The LSNB publishes the data to make it available to other CSLSs on the LSN. The LSNB is available only as a redundant pair.

GSN

Global Safety Network

The GSN is the network of connected LSNBs.

domain (GSN)

A GSN domain is a collection of LSNBs that can share secure parameters. This release supports only one domain so all LSNBs in the configuration are in that domain.

publisher

Publishers are logical entities in the DeltaV database. Publishers are not configured explicitly. As secure parameters within an LSN are referenced by other LSNs, the publisher creates and populates maps of secure parameters and references. The maps can be viewed from DeltaV Explorer.

safety system

In the context of CSLSs, safety system is shorthand for the CSLS safety system: the collection of all SIS controllers and CSLSs and other hardware and software.
