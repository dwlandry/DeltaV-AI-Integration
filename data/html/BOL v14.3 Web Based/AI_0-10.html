AI 0-10 VDC isolated CHARM specifications

Installation notes

  • This CHARM requires a CHARM Terminal Block.
  • Installing a 250 Ω resistor across terminals 3 and 4 converts a 4-20 mA field signal to a 1-5 VDC input signal.
