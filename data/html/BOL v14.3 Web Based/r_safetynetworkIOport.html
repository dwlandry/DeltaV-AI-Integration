Safety Network Ports specifications

Installation notes

  • The CHARMs Smart Logic Solver Carrier houses a primary and secondary Safety Network Port (SNP) for connections to the Local Safety Network.
  • The SNPs are removable. To remove a SNP, depress the button next to the SNP and push it out of the carrier.
