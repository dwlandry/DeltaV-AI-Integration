Automatic Upgrade of LSNBs and LSNGs

The LSNB and LSNG can be upgraded by Automatic Standby Upgrade. An Automatic Standby Upgrade changes the revision of a standby card to match the revision of the active card. If a standby card is inserted and the revision of the standby does not match the revision of the active, then the standby changes its revision to match the active card if the following conditions are met:
  • AutoUpgrade is supported in both the active and standby.
  • The AutoUpgrade option was selected in the LSNB or LSNG Properties Dialog.
  • The LSNB or LSNG is commissioned and downloaded.
  • The Keylock on the carrier is in the Lock position.
  • The software Major, Minor, or Maintenance revisions do not match. This does not include the build number.
  • The secondary Ethernet Isolation Port must be installed in the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.

The automatic upgrade feature only upgrades the components in the standby that are being used. When the upgrade is complete, the running component revisions of the active and standby match, and the standby unit becomes configured and available.
