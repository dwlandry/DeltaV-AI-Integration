SZ Controller specifications

Installation notes

  • Redundant SZ Controllers can install on the SZ Controller Carrier or on the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • SZ Controllers receive redundant 24 VDC input power through the SZ Controller Carrier or the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • SZ Controllers connect to the Area Control Network and to the Local Safety Network through the Ethernet Isolation Ports (primary and secondary) on the SZ Controller Carrier or the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • Redundant Modbus TCP ports on the SZ Controller Carrier enables the SZ Controller to support standalone SIS installations.
  • A physical keylock switch on the SZ Controller Carrier along with an unlock software function restricts how and when the associated CSLSs can be unlocked for downloads and upgrades.

Specifications

SZ Controller specifications
Item Specification
Input power requirement through the SZ Controller Carrier or Dual Universal Safety Carrier +24 VDC ±10% at 325 mA maximum for simplex; 575 mA maximum for redundant.
Ethernet connections through the SZ Controller Carrier or Dual Universal Safety Carrier Redundant 10/100BASE-TX ports.
Heat dissipation 7 W simplex; 13 W redundant
Isolation None. The SZ controller is referenced to the incoming 24 VDC return.
Mounting Horizontally in two slots on the SZ Controller Carrier or the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
