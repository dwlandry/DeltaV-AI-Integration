Simplex 16-Channel Analog Output Terminal Block

Installation Notes

  • The simplex 16-Channel Analog Output Terminal Block provides wiring terminations for the AO, 16-Channel, 4-20 mA HART, Series 2 Plus card in simplex mode.
  • Set the keys on the terminal block to A5 to match the AO, 16-Channel, 4-20 mA HART, Series 2 Plus card.
