Install local peer bus extender cables

Notice that the local peer bus extender cable has black and white boots. The cables connect black-to-black (D) and white-to-white (C).

  1. Place the cable end onto the BNC connector on the one-wide carrier.
    One-wide carriers


  2. Push and turn to lock the cable into place.
