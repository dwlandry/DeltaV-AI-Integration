Configuring areas

Areas as currently used within the DeltaV Explorer are for logical groupings of control strategies. Areas are not class-based objects. Areas do not share common properties since areas by themselves do not have properties. However, areas are used when configuring your batch system using unit modules and phase algorithm definitions. Areas are also used to group modules together.

The DeltaV system supports multiple areas. A typical use of areas is to group like control modules and unit modules in an area and then assign the unit modules to the appropriate process cell depending on the product to be made. For example, create an area for blenders. Place all the unit modules and control modules for blenders in that area. Assign unit modules used in your paint blending process to the Paint_Blend process cell.

For increased manageability, you assign areas to a specific Batch Executive. An area can be assigned to only one Batch Executive at a time. There can be up to four enabled Batch Executives in a DeltaV system.

User security is based on areas. A user, such as an operator, can have operation keys (BATCH_ADD, for example) assigned site-wide or by specific areas. This is important when designing your recipes and equipment. Operators can only load recipes and select equipment in areas where they have the appropriate privileges.

When the workstation has the option enabled for Restrict on-line changes to areas assigned to the Alarms and Events subsystem of this workstation, all areas assigned to that Batch Executive must also be assigned to the workstation's Alarms and Events subsystem.
