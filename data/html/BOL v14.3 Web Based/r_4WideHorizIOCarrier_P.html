4-Wide Horizontal I/O Interface and Power Carrier

PK 4-Wide I/O Carrier specifications
Item Specification
Capacity Four I/O cards with terminal blocks.
Maximum current

LocalBus (powers I/O cards): 8 A. For large systems, use the LocalBus extenders to add more power.

Bussed field power bus (per I/O card): 3.2 A at 30 VDC or 250 VAC for each connection.

LocalBus cable lengths

0.87 m (2.8 ft)

1.2 m (3.9 ft) (standard)

1.53 m (5.0 ft)
