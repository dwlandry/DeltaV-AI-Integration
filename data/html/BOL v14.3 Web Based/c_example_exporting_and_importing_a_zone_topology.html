Example: exporting and importing a Zone topology

For example, If three DeltaV systems (ZONE_A, ZONE_B, and ZONE_C) are connected with an Inter-Zone Control Network and you want alarms and events from at least some of the areas in each system to appear in other systems, or you want alarms to appear in alarm banners and displays, the general procedure to fully configure Local Shared Areas and Remote Zone Areas in all three systems is:

  1. From ZONE_A's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Create ZONE_B and ZONE_C under the Inter-Zone Server
    2. Share local areas in ZONE_B and ZONE_C
    3. Export the ZONE_A zone topology
  2. From ZONE_B's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Create ZONE_A and ZONE_C under the Inter-Zone Server
    2. Share local areas in ZONE_A and ZONE_C
    3. Export the ZONE_B zone topology
  3. From ZONE_C's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Create ZONE_A and ZONE_B under the Inter-Zone Server
    2. Share local areas in ZONE_A and ZONE_B
    3. Export the ZONE_C zone topology
  4. From ZONE_A's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_B
    2. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_C.
  5. From ZONE_B's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_A.
    2. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_C.
  6. From ZONE_C's ProfessionalPLUS workstation:
    1. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_A
    2. Import the zone topology exported from ZONE_B.
