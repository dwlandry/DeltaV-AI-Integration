Local Safety Network Bridge specifications

Installation notes

Note: These specifications also apply to the Local Safety Network Gateway.
  • Redundant Local Safety Network Bridges (LSNB) install on the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • Local Safety Network Bridges receive redundant 24 VDC input power from the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
  • Local Safety Network Bridges connect to the Global Safety Network and to the Local Safety Network through the Ethernet Isolation Ports (primary and secondary) on the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.

Specifications

Local Safety Network Bridge specifications
Item Specification
Input power requirement through the Dual Universal Safety Carrier +24 VDC ±10% at 325 mA maximum for simplex; 575 mA maximum for redundant.
Ethernet connection through the Dual Universal Safety Carrier Redundant 10/100BASE-TX ports.
Heat dissipation

7 W simplex; 13 W redundant

Isolation

None. The LSNB is referenced to the incoming 24 VDC return.

Mounting Horizontally in two slots on the Dual Universal Safety Carrier.
